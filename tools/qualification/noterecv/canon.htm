Modifications
X11 rows translated down one row. Spleen supplies lowercase, digits, braces and O,
except original TEC-OS i/t. One pixel removed from each e/r shoulder and t tip.
DEL retained unchanged. This is a research export, not product qualification.

Selected derivative dd42012c, exact export at Origo 77d828b9.
X11 rows lowered one row; Spleen digits, braces, O and lowercase; original i/t, followed by the three chosen pixel removals e(3,2), r(3,2), t(4,5). Original DEL retained.
Retained sources are under format/tec-compact; original source/bytes are format/font5x7-original.txt and format/font-original.bin.
